About Me

I am a game developer, programmer, and software developer graduating from Full Sail University. I have experience with C++, C#, Java, Unreal Engine 5, Unity, artificial intelligence, networking, and gameplay development.

I particularly enjoy level design, gameplay behavior, and developing level concepts. I enjoy combining programming and design to create engaging gameplay experiences, from building gameplay systems and AI behaviors to designing the environments in which those systems come together.
